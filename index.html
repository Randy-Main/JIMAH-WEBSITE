01 — ABOUT JIMAH

Building organizations that are ready for tomorrow.

J JIMAH CONSULTANCY
Professional Partnership Kenya

JIMAH CONSULTANCY SERVICES is a Kenyan professional consultancy firm providing practical, innovative and client-focused solutions to organizations.

We support businesses, cooperatives, SACCOs, SMEs, institutions, non-governmental organizations and other organizations to improve their strategy, people, operations, governance, financial sustainability and overall performance.

Our philosophy is simple: we help organizations turn ideas and challenges into practical, sustainable results.
